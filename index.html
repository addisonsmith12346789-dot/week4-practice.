Explore the Haunted Side of Virginia

Welcome to Hauntings in Virginia, a website dedicated to exploring Virginia's most mysterious and reportedly haunted locations. From historic buildings and abandoned places to legends passed down through generations, Virginia has many stories waiting to be discovered.

Our goal is to share information about haunted locations, local legends, reported paranormal activity, and the history behind some of Virginia's most mysterious places.

Explore our haunted locations to learn more about mysterious places throughout Virginia. You can also view photos in our gallery or contact us with a haunting story of your own.
