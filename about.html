About Hauntings in Virginia

Hauntings in Virginia was created for people who enjoy paranormal stories, Virginia history, abandoned locations, and unexplained events. Virginia has a long history filled with old towns, historic buildings, battlefields, cemeteries, and places connected to mysterious legends.

This website brings together information about reportedly haunted locations throughout the state. Our goal is to explore the history and stories behind these places while encouraging visitors to respect private property, local rules, and the safety of others.

Some stories are based on local legends or personal experiences and may not be scientifically verified. We encourage visitors to learn about the history of each location and consider different explanations for reported paranormal activity.
